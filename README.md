# CivicConnect AI 🚀

## Smart Public Grievance & Resolution Platform

CivicConnect AI is an AI-powered web platform that helps citizens report civic problems such as damaged roads, garbage, drainage issues, water problems, and broken streetlights.

The platform assists in identifying the complaint category, analyzing uploaded civic issue images, assigning priority and responsible departments, and tracking complaint resolution.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Citizens often face difficulties while reporting civic problems because they may not know the correct complaint category or responsible department. They also have limited visibility into the status of their complaints.

CivicConnect AI provides a centralized digital platform for reporting, AI-assisted classification, authority processing, and real-time complaint tracking.
